# Converted archive decisions keep their sections and alternatives inline

## Decision

Each archived decision D-NNN becomes one `decision-corpus` note with id suffix `R<NNN>` (the archive number, so `…-R006` is D-006) and alias `D-NNN`. The archive's Context, Decision (plus any extra labelled sections, as subsections), Alternatives and Consequences are carried over verbatim, with every `D-NNN` mention turned into a link. Alternatives stay inline with their recorded losing reasons — no separate alternative notes for the ~60 archived alternatives. Authority: `ruled-by-person` where the archive records the user's requirement, choice or confirmation; `judgement-by-agent` where it does not (D-001, D-008, D-015). Partial supersessions get two-way `supersedes`/`superseded_by` links, a "Superseded in part" line and a DEAD marker on the replaced claim.
